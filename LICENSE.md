## Generic-food reference database

`reference-foods.sqlite` is a separate database. Every row retains its source
prefix, and the database metadata records the exact source releases. It combines:

- **BLS 4.0, Max Rubner-Institut (2025)** — licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **USDA FoodData Central Foundation and Survey Foods** — US Government work
  released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (CoFID)
  2021, Public Health England** — contains public sector information licensed
  under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
- **Anses. 2025. Table de composition nutritionnelle des aliments Ciqual** —
  reused under France's [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/).
- **日本食品標準成分表（八訂）増補2023年, Ministry of Education, Culture,
  Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT), Japan** — reused under the
  [MEXT food-composition data-use statement](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/syokuhinseibun/), with the
  requested attribution `出典：日本食品標準成分表（八訂）増補2023年`.
